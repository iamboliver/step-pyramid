About the Pyraminx solver

The Pyraminx is a tetrahedral twisty puzzle invented by Uwe Mèffert in 1970 and one of the best-selling puzzles after the Rubik's Cube. Step Pyramid solves both the original 3-layer Pyraminx (4 tips, 4 corners and 6 edges) and the larger 4-layer Master Pyraminx, which adds a set of centre and "wing" pieces and needs wide 3-layer turns as well as the usual 1- and 2-layer twists.

Colour in each of the puzzle's 4 faces to match your scrambled Pyraminx, click Solve Pyraminx, and the solver works out an exact sequence of moves — then walks you through each one with a rotating 3D diagram and plain instructions for how many layers to grip and which way to turn. No account, no download, runs entirely in your browser.

Frequently asked

How many pieces does a Pyraminx have?
The standard 3-layer Pyraminx has 14 movable pieces: 4 tips, 4 corners and 6 edges. The 4-layer Master Pyraminx has 30: 4 tips, 4 corners, 4 centres, 6 middle edges and 12 "wing" pieces.
Is the Pyraminx easier than a Rubik's Cube?
Generally yes for the standard 3-layer version — the 4 corner tips can be twisted freely without affecting the rest of the puzzle, and there's no parity to worry about. The 4-layer Master Pyraminx is noticeably harder.
Does this work for a scrambled Pyraminx I already own?
Yes. Colour in each face exactly as your physical puzzle shows it — the solver reads the colours you enter, works out the solve from scratch, and doesn't assume any particular colour scheme or starting orientation.
